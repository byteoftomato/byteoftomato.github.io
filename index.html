About Me

Background

My interest in STEM started early, after meeting astronaut Dr. Roberta Bondar in elementary school and becoming fascinated by astronomy. I also grew up reading about women like Ada Lovelace and Mary Jackson who inspired me to explore engineering. In high school, that interest expanded into programming through web development with Girls Who Code and game development in computer science class. In university, I’ve gravitated toward the parts of computer engineering where code meets electronics, from digital systems and microcontrollers to embedded development and vehicle technology.
